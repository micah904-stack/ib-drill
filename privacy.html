Privacy Policy

IB Drill · Last updated: November 1, 2026

IB Drill ("the app") is a study tool that helps students practice investment banking interview questions. It is published by Micah Bozof. This policy explains what information the app handles.

Information we collect

We do not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information. The app does not require an account, does not ask for your name or email, and does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Information stored on your device

To remember your study progress and settings, the app saves small amounts of data (such as which questions you've reviewed) in your device's local browser storage. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data or your browser's site data, or by uninstalling the app.

Third parties

The app is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log basic technical information (such as IP addresses) when the app is loaded, as described in GitHub's Privacy Statement. We do not receive or use that information.

Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to micahb@terpmail.umd.edu.
